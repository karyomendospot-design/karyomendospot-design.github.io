LokaServe — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Privacy Policy

LokaServe ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting Pioneer data within the Pi Network ecosystem.

  • Information We Collect: When you sign in using Pi SDK, we access your Pi username and payment authorization solely to process service bookings. Booking details (name, contact number, service address, and schedule) are stored locally on your device to display your order history.
  • How We Use Data: Collected details are used strictly to facilitate local service requests and verify blockchain down-payment transactions (TXID). We do not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers.
  • Blockchain Transparency: Pi Testnet/Mainnet transaction IDs are recorded on the public Pi blockchain ledger.

2. Terms of Service

By accessing and using LokaServe inside the Pi Browser, you agree to the following terms:

  • Service Platform: LokaServe acts as a local service directory connecting Pioneers with independent service providers (AC technicians, handymen, and home cleaning).
  • Pi Payments: Down-payments (DP) made via Pi Wallet are processed through the official Pi Network SDK. Users are responsible for verifying order details before confirming transactions.
  • Testnet Phase: During Pi Hackathon and Testnet operations, transactions use Test-Pi without real-world monetary value.
